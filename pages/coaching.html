FAQ

What happens during the first consultation?

It's a two-hour strategic conversation — either in person at 67 Pall Mall or via Zoom. We'll explore what's really going on, what you want to change, and whether we're the right fit. No sales pressure.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Most clients work with me for 3-6 months, though some choose to continue longer. The duration depends on your goals and the depth of change you're seeking.

Is this therapy or counselling?

No. This is strategic coaching. While we may touch on emotional patterns, the focus is on forward movement, decision-making, and measurable outcomes.

What's the investment?

Investment details are shared after the initial consultation, as each engagement is tailored. The consultation itself is an investment in clarity.
